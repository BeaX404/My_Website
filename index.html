I've been drawing for a long time and the beautiful thing about drawing is you always learn something new.
From drawing you could eventually branch into other fields of interest like animation, comic making, graphics design,
character design, game design and so many others. Drawing is a skill. If you don't work on it consistently, you eventually do not develop it or it becomes rusty. But with drawing you can bring your imaginations to reality. You can reproduce them from your mind's eye. But anyone can draw and everyone has different drawing styles and art syles. Art styles are so beautiful and diverse. In my opinion, it portrays the artist's style and behaviour. Another thing is coloring. Coloring is not as easy as it looks. Everything colored must blend, not technically or literally, but as in the art should 'flow'.

Goals

Technology fascinates me. It intrigues me. Ever since I was a kid, I've been inspired but so many cartoons because of the level of technology in them. And not just cartoons. Stories. The level of technology portrayed is literally out of this world. Interstellar or space travel, hovercars, interactive holograms and so much more. As a kid, my initial plan was to follow in weapons technology partly because its cool and Ghana is severly lacking in firepower. I always had a vision that Ghana would reach the technological level ofWakanda from Marvel Avengers. And I am also interested in drone technology inspired by Dr. Robotnik from Sonic Hedgehog's drone technology. I also aim to work in the world of embedded systems although not fully and partly in game development. But my all time inspiration has been giant robots called 'Mechas' from movies like Transformers, Gangnam, Godzilla and others.
In summary, I want to be involved in the development of technology and the creation of new technologies. I want to be a part of the technological revolution that will shape the future of our world.
